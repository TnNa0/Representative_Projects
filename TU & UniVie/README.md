Self-Organizing Systems (SOS) – TU Wien

A collection of three group assignments exploring self-organizing systems and nature-inspired computational methods.

The projects cover Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), Swarm Intelligence, and Self-Organizing Maps (SOM), focusing on decentralized problem-solving, emergent collective behavior, heuristic optimization, and unsupervised learning.
